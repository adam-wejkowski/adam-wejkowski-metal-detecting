Metal Detecting

This hobby is about finding old relics, like coins or other objects that people used a long time ago, with a metal detector. It’s my favourite hobby because I really like finding old relics from the past. These finds are witnesses from the past. They can tell a whole story about the people who used them.

Read more about metal detectors

What I need for metal detecting:

  1. Metal detector
  2. Shovel
  3. Headphones
  4. Pinpointer
  5. Lots of pockets for the finds ;)

My favourite finds:

  • Old coins
  • Old relics like old axes
  • Plenty of old buttons

I usually search in fields or forests, and I have found lots of relics there. I’m really excited when I find something old. It makes me very happy, and when I realise what it is, I feel very proud.
